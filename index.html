What Is Data Analytics?

The Skill Every Industry Runs On

Data analytics is the process of collecting, cleaning, and examining data to uncover insights that drive smarter business decisions. From tracking sales trends to predicting customer churn, analysts turn raw numbers into strategy — across finance, healthcare, retail, marketing, and beyond.
